# Gratis POS — Free Point of Sale System

**GratisPOS** (also searched as **"Free POS"**) is a free, open-source Point of Sale (POS) system for small businesses, retail shops, and pharmacies. It handles sales transactions, inventory, purchasing, and reporting — all in one lightweight app you can download and run at no cost.

> 🔗 **[Download the latest version](https://github.com/pharmyohanaa-max/gratispos/releases/latest)**

---

## Why Gratis POS?

- 100% free to download and use
- Built for small shops, retail stores, and pharmacies
- Simple, fast interface for everyday sales
- Actively maintained with regular updates

## Features

- **Sales & checkout** — fast, smooth sales transactions at the counter
- **User login & selection** — secure, simplified login and cashier/user switching
- **Product management** — add and organize products, categories, and vendors
- **Purchasing & receiving** — create purchase orders and receive stock from suppliers
- **Inventory tracking** — a built-in ledger tracks stock movement for every product
- **Receipts** — view past sales by date and reprint any receipt on demand
- **Reporting** — accurate, up-to-date sales and inventory reports
- **Performance & security** — optimized for speed, stability, and reliability
